Volunteers support Think4U's education, community welfare, environment, skills, and outreach activities. Opportunities depend on current programs and the team's requirements.

## How to express interest

1. Explore the [program guide](/docs/programs) and choose the areas you would like to support.
2. Open the [official volunteer page](https://think4u.org/volunteer). It may ask you to sign in or create an account before continuing.
3. Complete the information requested through the official flow. Useful details include your skills, interests, location, and availability.
4. Wait for the team's instructions and confirm the activity, meeting arrangements, and role before attending.

For access problems or questions about opportunities, use [Contact Think4U](/docs/about/contact). Registration does not automatically guarantee acceptance, appointment, or participation.

## Ways to help

Depending on current needs, volunteering can involve educational activities, organizing materials for a drive, awareness campaigns, environmental work, student outreach, or offering relevant professional expertise. Ask the team which roles are available rather than assuming a particular activity is open.

College students can also explore the [Campus Ambassador Programme](/docs/participate/campus-ambassadors).

## Volunteer WhatsApp group

Use the current group route on the official website or ask the volunteer team after expressing interest. Verify the group and organizers through official channels. Private invitation links and member lists are not reproduced in these guides.

Keep beneficiary details, identity documents, payment evidence, and other people's contact information out of group chats. Use private official support channels for those matters.

## Respect the communities you support

Follow the coordinator's safeguarding instructions. Treat beneficiaries with dignity, obtain the required permissions before photography, and do not publish identifying details of children or vulnerable people. Raise concerns through the [grievance route](/docs/policies/grievances).

Any fundraising support must use official Trust channels. Volunteers must not collect donations in personal accounts or for personal gain.

Sources: [Volunteer registration](https://think4u.org/volunteer), [Terms of Service](https://think4u.org/terms-of-service), [Governance & Risk policies](https://think4u.org/governance-policies).
