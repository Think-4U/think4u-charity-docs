The Campus Ambassador Programme helps college students represent Think.4U's social initiatives, connect student volunteers, and build practical leadership experience.

## Who the public program page invites

The page invites students pursuing undergraduate, postgraduate, diploma, or professional courses who are interested in social service and community welfare. Check the current application for specific eligibility and selection requirements.

## What ambassadors may do

- Share approved program and awareness information on campus.
- Help students discover volunteering opportunities.
- Coordinate educational sessions, community activities, and student teams.
- Connect the organization with college authorities and relevant student clubs.
- Support authorized drives using official fundraising channels.

## How to apply

1. Visit the [official Campus Ambassador page](https://think4u.org/campus-ambassadors).
2. Read the current responsibilities and application instructions.
3. Follow the application form linked from that page. The reviewed page links to Google Forms.
4. Submit only the details requested by the official application and wait for the team's response.

Start from the official page to avoid stale or unofficial form links. Do not submit an application in this documentation site or pay a personal account claiming to secure selection.

## Recognition and expectations

The public page describes leadership experience, networking, and recognition. Ask the program team about attendance, service records, certificate criteria, duration, and current availability. This guide does not guarantee appointment, a certificate, employment, or an academic benefit.

For student entrepreneurship learning, see the [Entrepreneurship Mission](/docs/participate/entrepreneurship).
