Think4U invites collaboration with educational institutions, organizations, NGOs, community groups, and professionals. Its public partnership page describes education, women empowerment, youth development, community welfare, and entrepreneurship as areas of interest.

## Possible forms of collaboration

| Your organization or expertise | Topics to discuss |
| --- | --- |
| College or university | Student engagement, entrepreneurship learning, campus activities, and volunteering |
| Company or institutional supporter | Relevant community projects, skills, resources, and reporting expectations |
| NGO or community group | Joint outreach, local needs, and complementary activities |
| Professional or mentor | Education, guidance, or expertise relevant to a specific program |

## Prepare your proposal

Use the enquiry form on the [official partners page](https://think4u.org/partners) or [contact the office](https://think4u.org/contact). A useful initial enquiry includes your organization's name, your role and official contact, the proposed area of collaboration, location, intended contribution, and likely timing.

Start with a short summary. Do not attach confidential business information, beneficiary records, identity documents, or private contact lists to an initial proposal.

## Confirm arrangements before announcing a partnership

Discuss scope, responsibilities, permissions, reporting, safeguarding, and any formal documentation with the Think4U team. Submitting an enquiry does not establish an approved partnership or permission to use the organization's name or logo.

The public website discusses CSR collaboration. This guide does not certify eligibility for any statutory CSR arrangement, grant, or tax benefit. Verify applicable requirements and documentation directly before making commitments. [Current donation restrictions and 80G status](/docs/donations/eligibility-and-tax) also apply to proposed contributions.
