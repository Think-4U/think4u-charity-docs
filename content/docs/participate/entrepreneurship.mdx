Think4U describes its Entrepreneurship Mission as a free initiative for college students, combining entrepreneurship education, practical learning, and mentorship. It aims to help students develop ideas and explore businesses or social enterprises.

## What the curriculum covers

The public page groups learning around finding problems and ideas, understanding customers, planning a business, financial basics, digital communication, leadership, and sustainable growth. It also describes practical projects and guidance from mentors.

Its learning progression is **Learn → Think → Create → Build → Launch → Grow**. This is the program's stated learning journey, rather than a promise that every participant will launch a venture or receive investment.

## How to enquire

1. Read the [official Entrepreneurship Mission page](https://think4u.org/entrepreneurship).
2. Use the enquiry route linked there or the [official contact form](https://think4u.org/contact).
3. Mention your college, course, area of interest, and what you hope to learn. A short, non-confidential description of an idea is enough for an initial enquiry.
4. Ask about the next intake, eligibility, session format, timing, and participation requirements.

The public page says you do not need an already registered business to express interest. Do not assume an intake is open or a place is reserved until the team confirms it.

## Mentors and institutions

Educators, professionals, and institutions interested in contributing expertise can use the [partnership guide](/docs/participate/partners). Students interested in organizing campus engagement can explore [Campus Ambassadors](/docs/participate/campus-ambassadors).
