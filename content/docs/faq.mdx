## Can I donate from outside India?

Foreign contributions are not currently accepted. Think4U's FCRA application is pending, and the main website restricts access and donations to India. Read the [domestic donation notice](/docs/donations/eligibility-and-tax) and contact the team if you are uncertain about eligibility. Do not bypass the restrictions.

## Does my donation qualify for an 80G deduction?

Do not assume it does. Section 80G approval is under process. A donation receipt is not itself proof of deduction eligibility. Check the [current tax notice](https://think4u.org/tax-notice).

## Where do I donate?

Start with [think4u.org/donate](https://think4u.org/donate) or a campaign linked from the official website. Contributions must use official Trust channels. See the [donation guide](/docs/donations).

## Where is my receipt?

The main donation page directs donors to sign in for receipt access. If a receipt is missing or incorrect, follow the [receipt support guide](/docs/donations/receipts-and-refunds). This public documentation cannot retrieve private records.

## Can I cancel a donation or get a refund?

Donations are generally non-refundable. The official policy allows review of limited circumstances such as duplicate charges or payment-processing errors. See [refund guidance](/docs/donations/receipts-and-refunds) before submitting a private request.

## How do I volunteer or join the WhatsApp group?

Use the [volunteer route](https://think4u.org/volunteer), sign in if prompted, and follow the team's instructions. Request the current group route through official channels. See the [volunteer guide](/docs/participate/volunteer).

## How do students apply?

The [Campus Ambassador guide](/docs/participate/campus-ambassadors) explains the official application route. The [Entrepreneurship Mission guide](/docs/participate/entrepreneurship) explains how to enquire about free student learning. Confirm intake and eligibility with the team.

## Can an institution collaborate?

Yes, the public website invites institutional and professional enquiries. Use the [partner guide](/docs/participate/partners) to prepare a proposal. An enquiry does not itself establish an approved partnership.

## Are all listed programs running now?

Availability varies. The website includes past activities, program descriptions, and proposed work. Confirm dates and current opportunities directly; see [programs](/docs/programs) and [events](/docs/programs/events).

## Where do I change consent settings or raise a privacy concern?

Use the Consent Settings control on the main website or its Cookie Policy page. See [privacy and consent](/docs/policies/privacy-and-consent) for privacy requests and [grievances](/docs/policies/grievances) for unresolved concerns.
