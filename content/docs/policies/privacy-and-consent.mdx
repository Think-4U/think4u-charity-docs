## Read the official privacy information

The [Privacy Policy](https://think4u.org/privacy-policy) describes information provided for accounts, donations, participation, and enquiries, together with related technical information. It explains the purposes, service providers, retention, and privacy-request routes.

When using the main website, submit only the information requested for the task. Do not send passwords, payment PINs, OTPs, or complete card details to support staff, volunteers, or social groups.

## Manage consent on the main website

Open think4u.org and use its **Consent Settings** control, or follow the consent control on the [Cookie Policy page](https://think4u.org/cookie-policy). That control belongs to the main website. The documentation link explains where to find it; it does not change a main-site preference from this domain.

The Cookie Policy describes essential functions such as sign-in and preference storage. It also explains the consequences of disabling cookies. Review that page for current tracking disclosures instead of assuming every third-party service uses the same settings.

These public documentation pages are separate from your main-site account. Do not paste personal records into documentation corrections, public issue discussions, or shared copies of a guide.

## Request access, correction, or removal

Use the [official contact form](https://think4u.org/contact) or [info@think4u.org](mailto:info@think4u.org). Explain what you are requesting and how the team can respond. The policy says identity verification may be needed and that applicable retention obligations can affect deletion requests.

Do not send identification documents with an initial enquiry unless the team requests them through an appropriate official route. For an unresolved concern, use the [grievance process](/docs/policies/grievances).

## Photographs and beneficiary dignity

For concerns about a photograph, video, or identifying information, provide the page address and a short description privately. Avoid forwarding the image or identifying a child in a public message. The team can advise on the information needed to assess a removal request.

Sources: [Privacy Policy](https://think4u.org/privacy-policy), [Cookie Policy](https://think4u.org/cookie-policy), [Grievance Redressal Policy](https://think4u.org/grievance-policy).
