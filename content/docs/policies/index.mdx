## Choose a practical guide

- For a payment or receipt issue, see [receipts and refunds](/docs/donations/receipts-and-refunds).
- For data or consent questions, see [privacy and consent](/docs/policies/privacy-and-consent).

- For suspected vulnerabilities, see [security reporting](/docs/policies/security-and-reporting).
