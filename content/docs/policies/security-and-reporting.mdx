---
title: Security and responsible reporting
description: Protect your account and report suspected security issues through the published policy.
---

## Use official channels

Begin donations and participation from think4u.org. Verify unexpected requests through the [official contact details](/docs/about/contact), especially requests to send money to an individual's account or to share a verification code.

Do not send passwords, OTPs, UPI PINs, recovery codes, CVVs, or complete card numbers in emails, forms, documentation comments, or group chats. Enter verification information only in the official flow you initiated.

For a suspected unauthorized payment, contact Think4U and your bank or payment provider promptly. Use the [payment support guide](/docs/donations/receipts-and-refunds) to prepare a private report.

## Report a suspected vulnerability

Read the current [Security Vulnerability Disclosure & Bug Bounty Policy](https://think4u.org/security-policy). Use the report contact specified there. If you cannot locate that route, ask [info@think4u.org](mailto:info@think4u.org) how to send the report privately.

Keep the initial report concise: describe the affected public page, the issue, its observed impact, and how the organization can contact you. Provide only the minimum evidence needed through the approved private channel. Do not publish exploit details, credentials, or personal information in a public documentation issue.

## Follow the policy's boundaries

This guide does not grant permission to test systems. The official policy defines authorized scope and reporting conditions. Do not assume that the documentation domain, related services, or third-party systems are included.

Avoid accessing other users' data, disrupting service, attempting fraudulent payments, or targeting staff and volunteers. If you unexpectedly encounter personal information, stop accessing it and report the issue privately rather than collecting or sharing more data.

## Recognition and rewards

The published policy makes recognition discretionary and does not guarantee a monetary bounty. Do not assume a certificate, payment, or public acknowledgment is owed for a report. Consult the current policy for the complete conditions and disclosure process.

Sources: [Security & Bug Bounty Policy](https://think4u.org/security-policy), [Terms of Service](https://think4u.org/terms-of-service), [Grievance Redressal Policy](https://think4u.org/grievance-policy).
