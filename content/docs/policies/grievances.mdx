Think4U's published grievance process covers donor, volunteer, beneficiary, and website-user concerns. Examples include payment issues, account or privacy questions, volunteer coordination, conduct, and content-removal requests.

## Submit a concern

Use the [online grievance route](https://think4u.org/grievance). It may require sign-in. If you cannot access it, use the [public contact form](https://think4u.org/contact) or email [info@think4u.org](mailto:info@think4u.org).

The policy also lists [ceo@think4u.org](mailto:ceo@think4u.org) for administrative / leadership contact and [+91 99591 28946](tel:+919959128946) as the public contact number.

## Include enough to understand the issue

Provide your name and a reply contact, the date, a clear factual description, the relevant page or program, and any previous support reference. For a payment concern, include the donation or transaction reference and amount privately.

Share only relevant evidence. Redact unrelated financial activity, other people's details, and unnecessary identity information. **Never include passwords, OTPs, UPI PINs, CVVs, or complete card numbers.**

## What the published process says

The policy describes acknowledgment, verification where needed, investigation, and resolution. It publishes an acknowledgment aim of **24–48 business hours** and an ordinary resolution aim of **7–15 working days**. These are stated aims rather than a guaranteed outcome or a promise that every matter will finish within that period. Check the [current policy](https://think4u.org/grievance-policy) for updates.

Keep the acknowledgment reference and include it in follow-up messages.

## Choose a private route for sensitive concerns

Report beneficiary safety, misuse of funds, privacy, or conduct concerns privately through the official contact channels. Do not identify affected people in public documentation or social discussions. The [Governance & Risk policies](https://think4u.org/governance-policies) describe the organization's confidential-reporting and safeguarding commitments.

For a technical vulnerability, follow the separate [security reporting guide](/docs/policies/security-and-reporting).

Sources: [Grievance Redressal Policy](https://think4u.org/grievance-policy), [Contact](https://think4u.org/contact), [Governance & Risk policies](https://think4u.org/governance-policies).
