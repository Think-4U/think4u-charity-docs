**Status reviewed: 1 November 2026.** Confirm the live notices before every donation; this page is a guide to Think4U's published position, not a determination of your individual legal or tax eligibility.

## Domestic donations only

Think4U currently accepts domestic donations from within India through Indian bank accounts and domestic payment methods. **International or foreign contributions are not currently accepted.**

The published notice states that the application for applicable **FCRA authorization has been submitted and is pending**. An application is not an effective approval. Do not treat Think4U as authorized to accept foreign contributions until the official notice confirms the required authorization is effective.

The main website uses location restrictions. Do not bypass access or payment restrictions. If you are abroad, uncertain about the source of a proposed contribution, or incorrectly restricted while in India, contact [info@think4u.org](mailto:info@think4u.org) before attempting payment. A domestic payment option by itself does not establish eligibility.

Read the full [India-Only Donation / FCRA Notice](https://think4u.org/fcra-notice).

## Section 80G approval is under process

Think4U's application / approval relating to **Section 80G is currently under process** with the relevant government authority.

- Do not assume a current donation qualifies for an 80G deduction.
- A donation acknowledgment or receipt confirms the contribution; it does not itself establish tax-deduction eligibility.
- Any future benefit depends on formal approval, its effective date, donor eligibility, and applicable documentation requirements.

Think4U does not currently guarantee a tax deduction. Consult your own tax adviser about your circumstances and read the [80G & Tax Deduction Notice](https://think4u.org/tax-notice).

## Where to check for changes

Use the live FCRA and tax notices linked above, rather than a forwarded message or old receipt. Questions can be sent through the [official contact form](https://think4u.org/contact).
