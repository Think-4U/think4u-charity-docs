Think4U's public website accepts one-time charitable donations. The donation page supports a general contribution and, where available, contributions associated with a listed cause or campaign.

## Check these notices first

**Domestic donations only:** foreign contributions are currently not accepted; the FCRA application is pending. **80G approval is under process:** do not assume your donation currently qualifies for an 80G deduction. Read [eligibility and tax notices](/docs/donations/eligibility-and-tax) before proceeding.

## Use the official donation flow

1. Open [Donate on think4u.org](https://think4u.org/donate), or follow an active campaign from [Fundraising](https://think4u.org/fundraising).
2. Read the cause description, amount, and applicable notices. Confirm the purpose before proceeding.
3. Provide the donor details requested by the official form. Complete the contact verification offered there. The public donation page requires verification before payment.
4. Review the consent statement and linked Terms, Privacy Policy, and Donation & Refund Policy.
5. Continue to the payment checkout. The published policy identifies Cashfree as the payment provider. Use only the domestic payment options actually available in checkout.
6. Keep the payment reference and confirmation. Sign in on the main website for receipt access as directed by the donation page.

Do not send money through this documentation site or a personal payment address shared in a message. Start from the official donation page each time.

## Payment options

The published policy lists UPI, cards, net banking, and approved domestic wallets, subject to availability. Selecting a payment option does not override domestic-source eligibility or the foreign-contribution restriction.

## After payment

A bank debit alone may not confirm the final donation status. If the payment is pending, failed, duplicated, or missing a receipt, follow the [payment support guide](/docs/donations/receipts-and-refunds) before attempting another contribution.

Donations are generally non-refundable, with limited exceptions in the official policy. Read those terms before paying.

Sources: [Donate](https://think4u.org/donate), [Donation & Refund Policy](https://think4u.org/refund-policy), [Terms of Service](https://think4u.org/terms-of-service).
