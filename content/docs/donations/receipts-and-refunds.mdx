## Find your receipt

Keep your payment confirmation, date, amount, and transaction reference. The public donation page directs donors to sign in on the main website for receipt access. Use the account associated with your contribution and follow the options shown there.

If a receipt is missing or incorrect, contact [info@think4u.org](mailto:info@think4u.org) or the [support form](https://think4u.org/contact). Do not post your receipt or transaction details in public comments.

A receipt is a donation acknowledgment. [80G approval remains under process](/docs/donations/eligibility-and-tax), so a receipt does not establish that a deduction is available.

## If money was debited but confirmation is missing

Check the final status in the official payment or account flow. Avoid repeatedly retrying a payment that may still be pending. Ask Think4U to verify the transaction using your reference, date, and amount.

| Situation | What to explain to support |
| --- | --- |
| Bank debit, no donation confirmation | Payment date, amount, reference, and the status you see |
| Possible duplicate payment | Both references and debit times, if available |
| Incorrect receipt details | The specific field needing correction and the donation reference |
| Suspected unauthorized transaction | Report promptly to Think4U and your bank or payment provider |

## Refunds and cancellations

The published policy says donations are **generally non-refundable**, and a successfully completed donation generally cannot be cancelled. A change of mind does not ordinarily qualify for a refund.

Think4U may consider cases such as duplicate charges, technical or payment-processing errors, or refunds required by law. Approval is subject to review; submitting a request does not guarantee a refund.

For a refund request, the policy asks for the donor's name, registered contact details, payment / order reference, payment date, exact amount, reason, and relevant evidence. Send these privately through the official support route. Redact unrelated transactions and unnecessary account information from evidence.

**Never include passwords, OTPs, UPI PINs, CVVs, or complete card numbers.**

Approved refunds normally use the original payment mechanism. Credit timing depends on the payment provider and banks; consult the [current refund policy](https://think4u.org/refund-policy) and your case response rather than assuming a guaranteed date.

## If the issue remains unresolved

Use the [grievance process](/docs/policies/grievances) and include any existing support reference to help the team connect your messages.
