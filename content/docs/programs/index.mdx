## Ask about support or participation

Use the [official program directory](https://think4u.org/programs) and [contact form](https://think4u.org/contact). Mention the initiative, general location, and the kind of help or participation you are asking about. Confirm eligibility, availability, dates, and any required arrangements directly with the team.

For an initial enquiry, avoid sending beneficiary names, medical records, identification documents, or detailed personal circumstances. The team can advise what information is needed through an appropriate private route.

## Support a program

Explore [volunteering](/docs/participate/volunteer), [partnerships](/docs/participate/partners), or [eligible domestic donations](/docs/donations). Before collecting or delivering goods, ask the team which items are needed and what arrangements apply.

Health and legal program descriptions provide a way to enquire about activities; they do not establish individual treatment outcomes or legal advice. Confirm the service and relevant professional arrangements directly.
