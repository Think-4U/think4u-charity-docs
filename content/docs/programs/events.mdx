## Find an event or activity

Use [Events](https://think4u.org/events) and [Previous Events & Gallery](https://think4u.org/previous-events) on the official website. The reviewed pages include past milestones and community initiatives; an entry in the gallery is not confirmation of a new event.

Before planning to attend, confirm the date, location, eligibility, registration requirements, and organizer instructions. If no suitable activity is listed, ask through the [volunteer route](/docs/participate/volunteer) or [contact form](https://think4u.org/contact).

## Published milestones

The website records its official website launch on **4 October 2026** and describes previous relief, school-support, women's wellbeing, and legal-awareness initiatives. Consult the original pages for the associated accounts and media.

Past dates remain historical. Proposed drives and earlier launch announcements should not be treated as current schedules.

## Photographs and videos

The official gallery and [Think.4U YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@Think.4U-h9g) provide public media. Viewing a photograph does not grant permission to reuse it or identify the people shown.

Ask for permission before republishing beneficiary images, especially images involving children or vulnerable people. Do not add names, addresses, school details, or other identifying information when sharing an activity. For a privacy or removal request, use the [grievance guide](/docs/policies/grievances).

## Want to organize a drive?

Discuss the proposal with the team first. Confirm branding, supervision, collection arrangements, and permissions before announcing an activity in Think4U's name. Institutions can start with the [partner guide](/docs/participate/partners).
