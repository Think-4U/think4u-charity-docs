## Explore published work

Think4U shares community activity descriptions on its [home page](https://think4u.org/#impact), [program directory](https://think4u.org/programs), and [previous events gallery](https://think4u.org/previous-events). These are the starting points for learning about its work.

## Read goals and outcomes separately

The website includes both impact statements and proposed activities or expected outcomes. A proposed number of school kits, volunteer-network size, or planting target should not be treated as a completed or independently verified result.

When reviewing a claim, look for the activity, reporting period, location at an appropriate level of detail, and supporting published account. This guide does not reproduce changing headline totals or present roadmap targets as audited achievements.

For a specific reporting need, ask the team what public activity reports or appropriate supporting material are available. Do not request public disclosure of identifiable beneficiary or donor records.

## Find a campaign

Visit [Fundraising](https://think4u.org/fundraising) for current campaigns. Availability changes; an empty campaign listing does not mean that a forwarded fundraising message is authorized. Verify any proposed contribution through the official website or [contact route](/docs/about/contact).

Where a general donation is available, read the [donation guide](/docs/donations) and [current eligibility notices](/docs/donations/eligibility-and-tax) before paying.

## Know what a contribution supports

Read the selected cause, campaign description, and donation terms. The Terms of Service explain that project changes, funding levels, operational circumstances, and legal requirements can affect how funds are applied. Confirm any specific restriction or reporting requirement with the team before donating.

Sources: [Think4U impact section](https://think4u.org/#impact), [About and proposed roadmap](https://think4u.org/about), [Fundraising](https://think4u.org/fundraising), [Terms of Service](https://think4u.org/terms-of-service).
