---
title: Suggest a correction
description: Help keep the public Think4U guides accurate without sharing private information.
---

If a guide has an outdated link, unclear instruction, or incorrect public detail, send a correction through the [official contact form](https://think4u.org/contact) or email [info@think4u.org](mailto:info@think4u.org).

## What to include

Include the documentation page address, the section that needs attention, your suggested wording, and a link to an official public source where available. For a changed program date or policy notice, link to the new announcement rather than sending private correspondence.

## Keep personal information private

Do not post donor records, beneficiary details, identity documents, financial statements, payment screenshots, login codes, or private group invitations as documentation corrections. Use the [payment support guide](/docs/donations/receipts-and-refunds) for transaction issues and the [grievance guide](/docs/policies/grievances) for personal concerns.

## Report security issues separately

Send suspected vulnerabilities through the [responsible reporting route](/docs/policies/security-and-reporting). Do not include exploit details, access credentials, or another person's information in a public correction.

Sources: [Contact](https://think4u.org/contact), [Privacy Policy](https://think4u.org/privacy-policy), [Security & Bug Bounty Policy](https://think4u.org/security-policy).
