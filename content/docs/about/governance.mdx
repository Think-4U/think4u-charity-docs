## Public governance commitments

The [Governance & Risk policies](https://think4u.org/governance-policies) describe ethical conduct, safeguarding, conflicts of interest, responsible use of charitable resources, and confidential reporting. The full policy is the reference for those commitments.

## Verify a donation request

Think4U's published policies prohibit collecting or diverting charitable funds for personal gain. Make contributions only through official Trust channels linked from the main website. Do not transfer money to a representative's personal account or wallet simply because they claim to work with Think4U.

To verify a representative, campaign, or partnership request, use [official contact details](/docs/about/contact) before proceeding.

## Raise a concern

Use the [grievance guide](/docs/policies/grievances) for concerns about conduct, misuse of funds, beneficiary dignity, or privacy. Share relevant facts privately with the organization, rather than posting private records or allegations in public documentation.
