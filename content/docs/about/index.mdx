Think4U Charity Organization, also presented as **Think.4U Charitable Trust** on its website, is a registered charitable trust based in Hyderabad, Telangana. It works toward a compassionate, educated, and self-reliant society through community participation.

Its public motto is **Think. Empower. Uplift.** The website also describes its approach as **Love in Action, Care with Compassion**.

## What the organization focuses on

| Focus area | Activities described by Think4U |
| --- | --- |
| Education and youth | Learning materials, school support, mentoring, digital literacy, and student development |
| Women and livelihoods | Skill development, vocational learning, entrepreneurship, and community support |
| Health and wellbeing | Preventive health, hygiene, nutrition, and wellbeing awareness initiatives |
| Environment | Tree planting, clean-water initiatives, and environmental awareness |
| Social welfare | Relief collections, essential supplies, and support for underserved communities |

The trust describes its intended beneficiaries as children from underserved backgrounds, women and youth seeking skills, rural and tribal communities, senior citizens, persons with disabilities, and institutions such as schools and shelter homes.
