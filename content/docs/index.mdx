**Think4U Charity Organization** is a community charity platform dedicated to education, empowerment, health, and measurable social impact. Think.4U is a registered charitable trust based in Hyderabad, Telangana, India. Its motto is **Think. Empower. Uplift.**

These guides help you understand the public website, choose how to participate, and find the right official contact or policy.

## Before you donate

**Domestic donations only.** International or foreign contributions are currently not accepted. The FCRA application has been submitted and is pending.

**Section 80G approval is under process.** Do not assume a current donation qualifies for an 80G deduction until statutory approval is effective. Always check the [current donation and tax notices](/docs/donations/eligibility-and-tax).
